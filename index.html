Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chatvo ("Chatvo", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Chatvo mobile application for Android (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

Chatvo is an entertainment and informational tool that produces AI-generated estimates about chat conversations you choose to import or describe. It is not a surveillance service and does not access anyone's account, device, or messages on its own.

1. Information we process

  • Conversation content you provide. When you import a chat export file or paste conversation text for analysis, that text is processed to generate the analysis. Chat content and messages you type to the in-app AI assistant are sent over an encrypted connection to our analysis backend and to our AI processing provider, Together AI (see "Third parties").
  • Details about the person you analyze. If you enter a name, phone number, country code, or relationship label for a conversation partner, these details are stored in our database (Google Firebase) so the App can organize your analyses.
  • Contacts. If you choose to pick a person from your contacts, the App asks for permission to read your contacts. Your contact list is read only on your device to show a picker; only the single contact you select (name and phone number) is stored as described above. We do not upload your contact list.
  • Purchase & subscription data. Managed by Google Play and our billing provider RevenueCat — e.g. product purchased, transaction identifiers, entitlement status, and your in-app coin balance. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Device & usage data. A random installation/device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language, time zone, in-app events (screens viewed, features used), and diagnostics.
  • IP address & approximate location. Your IP address and a coarse, IP-based country estimate, used to localize currency and phone formats and to prevent abuse. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Advertising identifiers. The Android advertising ID and install referrer may be used by advertising SDKs for install measurement and attribution. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders locally on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the analysis and features you request.
  • To process and validate purchases, subscriptions, and coin balances.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the App.
  • To measure app performance and marketing effectiveness.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third parties we share with

We share only what is necessary for these services to function:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — analytics and app data storage.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat — purchase processing and validation.
  • Together AI (Together Computer, Inc.) — AI processing. Conversation text you submit and messages you send to the AI assistant are sent to Together AI solely to generate your results, under its privacy policy.
  • ipinfo — IP-based country lookup.
  • Meta (Facebook) and TikTok SDKs — advertising measurement and install attribution.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

Conversation content submitted for analysis is retained only as long as needed to produce and return your results and is not used to build advertising profiles. Other data is retained while your installation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Your choices & rights

  • You can deny or revoke contacts and notification permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
  • You can request access to, or deletion of, your data by emailing us at the address below. Please include the email or details that help us locate your data.

6. Children

Chatvo is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (such as encryption in transit) to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
